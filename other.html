Then, at the end of the NCIS season 24 premiere, DiNozzo became the Director of NCIS. It was an interesting development, as DiNozzo continued to help with cases in the field while also running the show from his office. He brought his own vibe and personality, even adding a popcorn machine and record player to the space to stand out as Vance's NCIS replacement and make it clear he was here to stay, which NCIS showrunner Steven D. Binder also confirms.

Yet, during NCIS season 24, episode 5, he was nowhere to be seen. The team had a tricky case that involved an eight-year-old girl left on her own in her apartment. With signs of a struggle, there were fears that her mom had been taken, but as the case evolved, the team realized that her mom had chosen to leave her daughter to keep everyone safe. Chloe also became crucial to solving the case, as she spoke Mandarin and understood everything the criminals said at the beginning of the hour.

In the end, DiNozzo's presence wasn't even needed. This was an hour for Knight to connect to her own heritage and share stories about her father, even offering Chloe the opportunity to call whenever she wanted to speak Mandarin. However, when there is an absence, especially with a returning character, there are concerns about what this means for the future.

When Weatherly returned to NCIS, it wasn't immediately clear if it was as a recurring guest star or as a series regular. Then he was added to the main credits, and the show made it clear to settle in for a ride with him as the boss. However, this is the first time he has been absent since coming back, and it's normal to be a little worried about that.

The good news is that this isn't going to happen every episode. DiNozzo has made it clear to McGee that when he feels the weight of the director position, he will want to blow off steam by helping to solve a case. He is far more involved in cases than his predecessors ever were, and that sets a new tone for the series. In fact, it felt like DiNozzo had forgotten what the job of a director was when he first started getting involved.

While he was gone, he was not forgotten. Knight took young Chloe into his office, where Chloe was able to create a fort and enjoy DiNozzo's popcorn. There was even a mention of "Mrs. Boss," making it clear that Ziva and Knight are still in contact to help keep DiNozzo's diet on track, and it clears the path for Ziva and Tali to make a cameo on NCIS season 24 with this connection.

There was no official reason for Tony DiNozzo to miss the hour. Nor was there an official reason given for Weatherly to not appear. However, it's not unusual for a character to miss an episode here or there on network shows. NCIS has seen it happen plenty of times in the past few seasons, and most of the time, it's been linked to budget cuts. Instead of reducing episode counts by too many, creating a disappointing run of a season, shows have reduced the amount of times the main actors are physically on the screen to reduce pay.

It's also possible that this is linked to Weatherly appearing in an episode of NCIS: Origins. When Gibbs needed help to find the person who shot him, he knew that DiNozzo would be there, and sure enough, on NCIS: Origins season 3, episode 3, the two had a long-awaited reunion. Weatherly's contract will be for a set number of episodes, usually with a guaranteed minimum. An appearance on a franchised show could count toward that minimum, or it could have been negotiated at the time so that Weatherly didn't feel burned out from the series already.

The benefit of Weatherly's DiNozzo now being the Director of NCIS is that he can be absent without much of a discussion. He will have tasks to do at Naval bases, and he's already pointed out that he has a lot of meetings to attend. There are various situations to control. On top of that, Tali is in a boarding school in Boston, so he may want to take leave and visit her and Ziva.

While this is the first absence, there will likely be more as NCIS season 24 continues. For one, the guaranteed minimum contracts that the majority of TV shows now have set up for characters being absent here and there. There are going to be episodes when various characters are absent.

For DiNozzo, the absences make the most sense due to his position. While Rocky Carroll was a series regular, he wasn't in every episode physically, and his absence never needed an explanation. It was one of the reasons the idea of McGee being promoted to director didn't make sense, as it would mean less time with Sean Murray on the screen solving cases. As mentioned, a director has many other tasks to do, and it's easy for viewers to just assume this.

Tony Dinozzo Jr. has made his triumphant return to Navy Yard as Leon Vance's replacement, but it's curious what his fate is beyond NCIS season 24.

The good news is that DiNozzo is here to stay. At least, that's the case when it comes to NCIS season 24. The move to DiNozzo being in the director's office creates a new and mature Tony, allowing the other characters to see how much can develop when you're in another country and have to raise a child alone for some time.

As DiNozzo still likes to solve cases and uses it as a way to blow off some steam, it also means that when other characters are absent, there's someone competent to take on their work burden. For now, DiNozzo is likely to return on NCIS season 24, episode 6.

“What happens when children see these images? Neurological and psychological factors are affected, because they see body dimensions that are completely different from how they see themselves,” Milagros Jáuregui, a conservative senator from the Renovación Popular party, said in a speech on 26 October.

The sculptures are located in Parque de la Fertilidad (Fertility Park), a public space where in 2021 then-mayor Arturo Fernández of the Moche district began installing giant fibreglass reproductions of ceramic depictions of sexual acts to promote tourism.

Known as huacos eróticos, the much smaller original ceramics date from the 3rd to 8th century AD. No more than 30cm in height, they were placed in tombs as burial offerings. But the debate about their much larger versions in the park goes beyond political prudishness.

“It’s a complicated issue,” the Peruvian archaeologist Walter Alva tells The Art Newspaper. “There’s freedom to express or re-create themes from the past, but these ceramics were created to be placed as offerings to the dead, with a sense of revitalising life. They are associated with both fertility and an entire system of beliefs.”

An original huaco erótico ceramic offering made by the Moche civilisation, Larco Museum, Lima Photo: László Varga, via Wikimedia Commons

The Moche civilisation covered 700km of the coast of what is now Peru, between the regions of Piura and Ancash, centuries before the Inca dominated the area. “Moche art is primarily ceramic, and these vessels depict erotic scenes or concepts of sexuality from that era,” Alva says. “The sculptures in the park were created outside of their historical context.”

One of the greatest archaeological discoveries from the Moche civilisation was that of the Lord of Sipán near the city of Chiclayo—a discovery Alva made in 1987. A 3rd-century monarch, the Lord of Sipán’s body was found covered with gold along with eight other people, a dog and two llamas and nearly 500 objects including ceramic vessels, ornaments and textiles.

The Moche have the largest number of erotic representations among pre-Hispanic civilisations. The most complete collection of its ceramics is in Lima, at the Larco Museum of pre-Columbian art, which celebrated its centennial in July. Before the 1960s, children were forbidden from entering the room that housed the huacos. Today, minors must be accompanied by an adult. The institution’s Erotic Gallery is one of its most popular attractions.

Jáuregui, an evangelical pastor as well as a senator, believes these Moche sculptures should only be in closed spaces. “Displaying these types of images in public is harmful to children,” she told congress. “We should have them in museums, where high-school students can observe a part of our culture that is a national treasure with the help of teachers or some kind of tutor.”

The cutback from the soon-34-year-old didn't reach the midfielder, but the goalkeeper Katarzyna Kiedrzynek's attempt to block the ball resulted in an own-goal.

Surprisingly, PSG showed attacking intent after conceding the opener. That meant more spaces for Bayern to attack, and they doubled their advantage with a magnificent linkup play between Harder and Bühl after Momoko Tanikawa won possession in the final third.

Bühl, who didn't feature for the club since the matchday 3 draw with Real Madrid, scored her second Champions League goal of the season. Compared to the first-half, there were lots of actions in both boxes right after the restart. Kiedrzynek denied Bühl from scoring her tenth goal of the season with a decent save before her side halved the deficit.

Fellow Polish international Dudek scored from the penalty spot after Dallmann's handball twenty minutes into the second-half. Dallmann scored at the other end ten minutes before time, but it was from an offside position.

Despite their dominant display in the first 45 minutes, Bayern struggled for the majority of the second-half as the hosts looked to repeat what Manchester City did to the German giants in the opening matchday. However, the visitors managed to see out the win to momentarily climb to second place.

FC Bayern have a perfect record away from home as their next game will also be on the road against Austria Vienna before hosting the champions Barcelona in the last matchday. Jose Barcala’s side will face second-placed VfL Wolfsburg on Saturday as they aim to extend their lead in the Frauen-Bundesliga.
