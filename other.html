Rams sign tight end away NFC West rival’s practice squad

📅 Nov 28, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

The Los Angeles Rams are turning to a familiar NFC West rival for help at tight end. Following unfortunate injury news involving Davis Allen and Tyler Higbee, Los Angeles has signed veteran tight end Jack Stoll off the San Francisco 49ers' practice squad, according to NFL Network's Mike Garafolo.

The move comes at an important time for Sean McVay's offense, which has suddenly lost two of its key tight end options. Allen suffered a high ankle sprain that is expected to sideline him for multiple weeks, while Higbee is dealing with a chest contusion and is considered week-to-week.

With Terrance Ferguson already on injured reserve, the Rams needed additional depth behind Colby Parkinson and rookie Max Klare. Stoll provides an experienced option who can contribute as both a blocker and pass catcher.

Stoll originally entered the NFL as an undrafted free agent out of Nebraska in 2021, signing with the Philadelphia Eagles. He spent his first three seasons in Philadelphia, helping the organization reach Super Bowl LVII.

His career took several turns in 2024. After signing with the New York Giants that offseason, Stoll was released during final roster cuts. He returned to Philadelphia's practice squad before being eventually claimed off waivers by the Miami Dolphins.

In 2025, Stoll signed with the New Orleans Saints, where he appeared in 15 games, including four starts. He later signed with the Cleveland Browns in March 2026 but did not make their final roster. Afterward, he landed on the 49ers' practice squad in September before the Rams came calling on Thanksgiving.

Across 77 regular-season appearances, Stoll has recorded 28 receptions for 239 yards and one touchdown. His best statistical season came in 2022 with Philadelphia, when he caught 11 passes for 123 yards.

Stoll isn't expected to replace the receiving production of Allen or Higbee. However, his experience as a blocking tight end could prove valuable as the Rams navigate another difficult stretch of injuries.

It's only a matter of time. Stoll will probably be active for Week 13 but playing time will be determined based on how quickly he can acclimate with the playbook. Stoll's strength as a run blocker signals that Sean McVay is committed to 13 personnel and with the Rams only having Colby Parkinson and Max Klare, it is imperative that they have depth behind those two. Dan Villari could be an option as the third tight end if Stoll is not ready to go.

GTA 6: Should You Kill Or Spare Felipe In "Bad Driver"?

📅 Nov 28, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

One of the many optional missions in Grand Theft Auto 6 that involves a life-or-death decision is "Bad Driver!", which becomes available during Chapter 4 after completing the main story mission "Party Hard." Andrea will contact you regarding the whereabouts of Felipe, the getaway driver who abandoned the crew during the Cartel Heist, sending you to a bar in Millport, Lummox County.

You can complete this mission as either Jason or Lucia, though it's permanently missable if you progress to the "Power Play" mission before tracking Felipe down. After a brief chase into the woods, you'll have an opportunity to question Felipe about his actions before ultimately deciding his fate. There are substantially different rewards depending on whether you kill or spare Felipe, so choose wisely.

When you arrive at the designated bar in Millport during "Bad Driver!", head toward the back of the building, where Felipe will emerge from the bathroom and immediately make a run for it. You'll need to chase Felipe into the nearby woods, though you can make catching him considerably easier by shooting him in the legs to slow him down.

Once you've caught up to Felipe, hit him with a melee attack to force him to surrender, at which point you can question him about why he abandoned the crew during the Cartel Heist. You can continue interrogating Felipe to hear his side of the story, though you're free to shoot and kill him at any point during the conversation.

You can grab a Collectible Card for Braelynn in the broken down house along the path where you chase Felipe down.

Killing Felipe will earn you a $20,000 payout from Andrea, which is a pretty substantial reward considering how little work the mission requires. However, you'll permanently miss out on an additional optional mission and a vehicle reward that are only available if you let Felipe live.

If you decide to hear Felipe out and give him a second chance, you can select the dialogue option to spare him after questioning him about the Cartel Heist. Felipe will be free to go, and although you'll miss out on the $20,000 payout from Andrea, this isn't the last you'll hear from him.

Sparing Felipe unlocks an additional optional mission called "Collect Felipe's Gift." Felipe will contact you sometime after completing "Bad Driver!" and let you know that he's left you his vehicle as a reward for letting him live. You'll then be able to head to the designated location and collect the vehicle for yourself.

The vehicle in question is Felipe's Declasse '80 Impaler, a black sedan parked outside the chapel near Lost Heaven Cemetery in Millport. The vehicle doesn't have a tracker, so you can drive it straight to a Pay 'N' Spray and register it for $1,050. Unfortunately, the Impaler only sells for roughly $2,450, meaning sparing Felipe is considerably less lucrative from a purely financial standpoint.

Ultimately, you should spare Felipe if you're interested in experiencing as much optional content in GTA 6 as possible. While killing him is considerably more lucrative, sparing him unlocks an additional mission and gives you a vehicle to add to your collection.

There are plenty of other opportunities to make $20,000 throughout the game, whereas killing Felipe permanently locks you out of "Collect Felipe's Gift." However, if you aren't particularly interested in collecting vehicles or completing every optional mission in GTA 6, killing Felipe is easily the more financially rewarding option.

The Cowboys led 28-10 at halftime and 31-10 in the third quarter before allowing an Eagles team, which had lost seven of its previous eight games and playing without their starting quarterback in Jalen Hurts, Pro Bowl running back in Saquon Barkley and Pro Bowl cornerback Cooper DeJean, steal their glory.

It didn't help that the Eagles were led by backup quarterback Joe Milton, who was cut by the Cowboys in preseason and was signed to their NFC East Rivals last month spending the early part of the season the Dallas practice squad.

Milton passed for 331 yards with three passing touchdowns and one rushing touchdown. He led the Eagles on the game-winning drive that was culminated by a 59-yard field goal from Jake Elliott with two seconds left.

The Dallas Cowboys again failed to win back to back games. It is now a full calendar year since they won consecutive games. They failed to get back to .500 and they fell further behind the first-place New York Giants (6-3-1) in the NFC East playoff chase.

The Cowboys, who came into the season talking about reaching the Super Bowl for the first time since 1996, have already made a change on defense, taking play calling duties away from coordinator Christian Parker and giving them to senior assistant Mike Pettine.

So Jones remains firm in his decision to not make move on head coach Brian Schottenheimer during the season.

It was Schottenheimer's decision to go for it on fourth and 1 and the Eagles 39 with 55 seconds left, rather than punt or kick the field goal. Quarterback Dak Prescott was sacked for a 9-yard loss, setting Philadelphia up at the 48.

Seven players later, Elliott kicked the field goal sending a somber Jones into the Cowboys locker room, as miserable and despondent as he's ever been.

It was the third time this season, the Cowboys have lost a home game against the a seemingly overmatched opponent with a losing record, playing with a backup quarterback. It happened against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers and the Arizona Cardinals.

Now there is a question on whether Cowboys will win another game.

After a 11-day layoff, the Cowboys face the defending Super Bowl champion Seattle Seahawks (6-4) on the road on Monday night, followed bye a bye and a matchup at that the Los Angeles Rams (7-4) and then home game against the Jacksonville Jaguars (7-3) and Giants before ending the season at the Washington Commanders 4-5-1.

While he is not ready to make a coaching change with Schottenheimer, Jones was resolute in saying everything needed to be evaluated, including Schottenheimer.

Prescott, refused to talk about Schottenheimer job, saying he needed to be better and the offense needed to score more in the second half.

But he echoed Jones in saying he was numb to all.

"Pretty numb, yeah, that's a good description for how I'm feeling right now," Prescott said.

"It's pretty stunning," Prescott added. "I said that about the Cardinals game. But this one takes the cake."

The Cowboys were 63-1 when leading by 18 at the half in franchise history going into the game. They are now 63-2. The ultimate 21-point comebacker was the biggest comeback on Thanksgiving Day in NFL history.

Prescott seemingly did all he could as continued an amazing hot streak over the last month. He completed 10 of 29 passes for two touchdowns through the air and one on the ground before a hailmary interception on the last play of the game.

In his last six starts, Prescott has 20 touchdowns to one interception but the Cowboys are 3-3 in those games. Prescott has directed a Cowboys offense that has scored 40, 51 and 38 points the last three games has a 1-2 record to show for it.

Blame on Thursday goes go the special teams, which allowed a blocked punt for touchdown in the quarter and a missed 40-yard field goal by Brandon Aubrey that could have conceivably put the game away in the fourth quarter.

Also blame a defense that has been awful all season. It now given up 20 or more points in 19 straight games dating back to last season. The Cowboys now have the most home losses when scoring 30+ points (4) in a single season in NFL history.

The Eagles have fielded one of the worst offenses in NFL all season. They had not scored more than 24 points in a game. They drop 39 on Dallas with Milton, including 22 in the fourth quarter.

Most of the time, Superman is in good standing with the Green Lanterns, particularly the ones hailing from Sector 2814. Most of the Justice League’s origin stories depict the Man of Steel co-founding the superhero team with Hal Jordan, the first human to join the Green Lantern Corps. John Stewart, Guy Gardner, Kyle Rayner, Simon Baz, Jessica Cruz, and Jo Mullein all later joined the Justice League, so Superman has frequently teamed up with them as well.

His interactions with the Green Lantern Corps as a whole, on the other hand, have largely been confined to crossovers, like when many of these emerald ring slingers came to Earth to battle the Black Lanterns in the Blackest Night event. However, in a few months time, the Green Lantern Corps will have a major presence in the ongoing Superman series. Unfortunately for Clark Kent, he’ll find himself at odds with the Green Lanterns, which will set up a big showdown.

More than 15 years after Blackest Night ended, Superman is once again having to contend with the Black Lanterns and their leader, Nekron. The “Night Shift” storyline kicked off in Superman #43 with the title protagonist being blasted with energy from a corrupted Black Lantern Ring, which in turn led to him being burned by sunlight. Further experimentation in Issue #44 showed Superman being burned and even igniting into flame when exposed to sunlight, which he can also no longer absorb.

The solicitation for Superman #47 reveals that “Night Shift” will conclude this issue with Clark having to “stop the Green Lantern Corps from taking the Black Lantern Ring out of Metropolis.” The reason for this apparently has something to do with a ticking clock threat, as his allies Marilyn Moonlight and Silver Banshee must venture into Metropolis’ hidden Dead Zone to “find the Cult Leader” who worships Nekron and “free his zombie followers before the Dead Zone is cut off forever.”

Superman #47 will also tie into the wider DC All-In saga by featuring an appearance from Pandora, who was a major player during the New 52 era, and is now a member of Darkseid’s sinister Justice League. As far as the Green Lanterns go, the issue’s cover shows that Green Lantern Corps leader Jessica Cruz did not skimp on bringing reinforcements to confront Superman.

Before Hal Jordan was selected to become the Green Lantern of Sector 2814, there was another classic DC character who nearly got the ring instead.

Even assuming that Clark will be back to normal by Superman #47, there’s still the question of how he’ll fare at full power against the Green Lantern Corps. This kind of battle has never happened within the mainstream DC Universe, and while Superman can easily overpower one or two Green Lanterns on his own, an army of them might be too much, especially since their emerald energy was once shown to be capable of replicating Kryptonite’s radiation.

Plus, Superman’s against killing, so he’d have to restrain himself to a certain degree fighting off many Green Lanterns. However, that wasn’t a concern for a different, more brutal version of Superman. In Injustice: Gods Among Us Year Two, Clark joined forces with the Sinestro Corps to wage war against the Green Lanterns. Not only did he easily defeat many of them at his usual power level, he pushed Mogo and the Guardian Gantry into the Sun when he was further empowered by a Yellow Lantern ring.

There’s also the possibility that Superman’s confrontation with the Green Lantern Corps a few months from now won’t get too out of hand. If Marilyn Moonlight and Silver Banshee can pull off their rescue mission in time, then a fight won’t be necessary, and Superman won’t have any problem turning over the Black Lantern ring to the Corps. Still, it’s likely that in an action-packed comic like this, there will at least be a brief skirmish, albeit with no major casualties like what happened in the Injustice universe.

Superman #47 will be released Wednesday, February 24, 2027, by DC Comics.

These Rare Pokémon Cards Are No Longer Eligible for PSA Grading

📅 Nov 26, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

English-language Mega Hyper Rare cards have a manufacturing issue

Mega Evolution returned to the Pokémon Trading Card Game with the launch of the Mega Evolution series in 2025. In this expansion, collectors can find extremely rare Mega Evolution Pokémon EX cards known as Mega Hyper Rare cards in English and Mega Ultra Rare cards in Japanese. These holographic secret cards show alternative artwork of Pokémon's Mega Evolutions on a beautiful gold-etched display.

Given how rare these cards are, most collectors lucky enough to find them quickly sent them to PSA for grading. Unfortunately, in many cases, this will no longer be possible. On Nov. 25, 2026, the PSA announced that it has stopped grading English-language Mega Hyper Rare Pokémon cards effective immediately.

In an official blog post, PSA explains that some English-language Mega Hyper Rare Pokémon cards are defective, and therefore, none can be accurately graded. Card grading experts "identified a manufacturing issue that can cause the gold foil on the front of these cards to deteriorate over time, leaving visible patches or fading." While the severity of the defect varies from card to card, it's impossible to determine whether a card will eventually be affected by it and deteriorate. The issue can arise even if the card is properly stored and encapsulated.

"A card can begin deteriorating after it’s graded, even with no visible signs at first, so we can’t assign a grade that will reliably hold. Continuing to grade these cards wouldn’t meet the standard of accuracy and consistency that collectors rely on us for."

Grades already assigned to English-language Mega Hyper Rare cards by PSA will not be revoked and can be submitted for Review and Reholder service, allowing collectors to reevaluate their condition. Cards in transit or submitted for grading will be returned ungraded, and PSA will waive the grading service fee as compensation.

On the bright side, PSA states that Japanese-language Mega Ultra Rare Pokémon cards do not appear to be defective, as they were printed at a facility that uses different techniques, and can be submitted for grading for the time being.

It is currently unknown how this will affect card prices. It could go either way. The price of defective cards might rise before the manufacturing error is fixed, or it could fall sharply, as collectors begin their search for a pristine Japanese MUR card or look for an already graded English-language card instead.

The Jacksonville Jaguars will host the Tennessee Titans on Sunday, looking to rebound from their disappointing loss to the New York Giants and complete a season sweep of their AFC South rivals.

Although Jacksonville enters as the clear favorite, injuries along the offensive line and Tennessee's improving offense could present challenges. Furthermore, the Jaguars were favored against the Giants as well, and performed well below expectations. Will the same happen again in Week 12?

Jacksonville's offensive line will be under considerable scrutiny after its struggles against the Giants, particularly with injuries continuing to mount. Starting center Robert Hainsey missed the Giants game with a knee injury, while left tackle Walker Little suffered a knee injury during the game and did not return. With Anton Harrison already out for the season, Jacksonville could be forced to rely on Wyatt Milum at left tackle if Little cannot play. Patrick Mekari is starting the process of coming off injured reserve, but that doesn't mean he'll be playing on Sunday.

That makes Titans defensive end Jacob Martin an especially important player to watch.

Lawrence was sacked three times against New York and faced pressure throughout the game, including 11 quarterback hits. Jacksonville cannot afford a repeat performance, even against a Tennessee defense that has struggled considerably this season.

If anyone is looking for redemption on Sunday, it might be Parker Washington. He delivered a strong performance against the Giants, catching eight passes for 103 yards, but most notably had a catch that would have been a touchdown, except for referees ruling it incomplete. That call caused league-wide controversy, and Washington could understandably be looking to get some measure of payback with a big game.

Tennessee cornerbacks Alontae Taylor and Cor'Dale Flott, however, will be looking to prevent an explosive performance. The Titans have struggled considerably against opposing passing attacks, and the departure of safety Amani Hooker at the trade deadline has only made it worse.

He has racked up 738 rushing yards and six touchdowns so far this season.

The Jaguars, meanwhile, had some difficulty with their run defense in the previous Tennessee game. Although the Titans recorded just 105 rushing yards, they averaged an impressive 6.2 yards per carry. Pollard also caught a 32-yard touchdown pass that brought Tennessee within seven points during the fourth quarter.

Hines-Allen and Walker will need to maintain their rush lanes while generating pressure, preventing Ward from escaping the pocket and finding his receivers downfield.
