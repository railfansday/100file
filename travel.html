‘Not Served In Exit Rows’: United Passenger Refused A Drink Gets 5,000 Miles

📅 Nov 12, 2026  |  Category: Travel  |  Read Source →

An exit-row passenger was flying to Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU). While on the flight, the passenger asked for an alcoholic drink and was refused service. The flight attendant said that such beverages are not served to exit-row passengers.

After other Reddit users told the passenger that the flight attendant was not following Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) policy, they put in a complaint with the airline. United apologized and added 5,000 miles to the passenger's MileagePlus account. Simple Flying has contacted United, who have said they will not comment on the matter.

In their Reddit post on the Sub-Reddit r/unitedairlines, titled "No adult drinks in exit row," the passenger explains that they were flying to SJU for the evening before their birthday. To celebrate their vacation, the passenger tried to purchase an alcoholic drink on the United flight and was disappointed to be denied service. The passenger initially thought that the flight attendant was correct, writing: "bad time to learn that alcohol is not served in exit rows."

The passenger then gave three updates to the many other Redditors who commented. The first said that they had made a complaint. The second explained that their being drunk wasn't the reason they had been refused, as the drink they ordered would have been the first of the day. "I get that drunk passengers are a menace," they wrote. Finally, the passenger gave the update that they had received 5,000 United MileagePlus miles.

The compensation comes because the flight attendant broke United's alcohol sales policy. Alcoholic drinks are only not sold to those under 21 years old, or those who are already intoxicated. Furthermore, the FAA has no ban on serving alcohol in exit rows. As a result, there is no reason that the passenger should have been denied service.

There are some rules about exit row travel that frequent flyers will be aware of. Passengers in these rows need to be a minimum of 15 years old, physically mobile, and able to read and understand safety briefings. They also must be able to respond to oral instructions in English or the crew's operational language. Finally, passengers in the exit row can't have other responsibilities that would conflict with them helping in an emergency. Examples include that they are traveling with small children or service animals that are caring for another passenger.

As well as being able to help in an emergency, they must also be willing to do so. In the US, confirmation of this must be given verbally.

Delta and other carriers must start pre-departure service earlier so galley items are stowed prior to aircraft movement.

"With 5k miles, nothing. Just keep earning more," writes one Reddit user on a different thread, underlining that the miles given to the passenger impacted by the denial of service is an apology and a gesture rather than true compensation.

Having the 5,000 miles contribute towards a larger spend is not the only way that the passenger could benefit from their apology. They could use MileagePlus Merchandise Awards to shop for electronics, household items and jewelry, although their options would be limited to just 5,000 miles to play with. The only item currently available in the 3,000 to 5,000-mile range is a Gaiam Sidekick Waist Pack, costing 4,800 miles. This pack has a retail price of around $20. Other rewards, like hotels, car hire, and cruises, will also be out of reach with the low amount of miles.

However, some ways of using small amounts of miles are no longer in operation. For example, United used to have a program called MagsforMiles, where passengers could use their miles to take out magazine subscriptions. This program has now been shut down.

All quiver upgrade locations in Ocarina of Time

How to increase the number of arrows Link can carry

The quiver upgrades in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake improve one of Link's best weapons: the Fairy Bow. As adult Link, the bow is your strongest ranged weapon, especially after you have unlocked special types of arrows, such as the Fire Arrow. The only downside to using the bow is that Link carries a limited number of arrows — a drawback that you can solve by finding the right upgrades.

In this guide, we explain where to find all quiver upgrades in the Ocariana of Time remake.

When you get the Fairy Bow in the Forest Temple, Link's quiver has a base capacity of 30 arrows. You can increase this number by obtaining the Bigger Quiver and the Biggest Quiver upgrades. Each one increases the total number of arrows he can carry by 10, allowing you to carry up to 50 arrows after unlocking both upgrades.

You will find the first upgrade from Kakariko Village and the second in the Gerudo Fortress. You can go after the first upgrade as soon as you leave the Forest Temple, as there are no prerequisites to unlock it. On the other hand, before you can try to get the Biggest Quiver, you must have unlocked Epona, and rescued the carpenters to gain free access to the fortress.

Below, you'll find all the details on how to unlock each one of the upgrades.

You obtain the Bigger Quiver upgrade by playing in the Shooting Gallery minigame in Kakariko Village as adult Link. Talk to the Shooting-Gallery Operator and he will charge you 30 rupees to play. You must hit 10 targets to earn the upgrade. We recommend saving before starting the minigame so you can reload if you fail and don't want to pay the fee again.

While there's no timer forcing you to fire the arrows quickly, you have a total of 15 arrows to use, so take your time to aim at the moving targets. Most of them eventually stop in one place, giving more than enough time to line up your shot. After destroying 10 targets, the game ends and the Shooting-Gallery Operator rewards you with the Bigger Quiver upgrade.

To unlock the second quiver upgrade, you must travel to Gerudo Fortress to the west of Hyrule Field. On the southeastern side of it, you will find the Horseback Archery Range. Talk to the Horseback-Archery Attendant while riding Epona or with her nearby, and she will charge you 30 rupees to play the minigame.

The goal is to shoot the targets around the track while riding your horse to earn points. Score at least 1,400 points and the Biggest Quiver, along with a Piece of Heart, is yours. You have only 20 arrows, so every shot counts. Each pot on the ground and each hit right in the center of the large targets earns you 100 points.

American said it will operate 7,021 flights on the Sunday after Thanksgiving, Nov. 29, making it the airline’s largest operating day since the COVID-19 pandemic.

A beloved Doctor Who episode is now inaccessible due to allegations surrounding guest star

Following the publication of allegations of sexual misconduct against British actor David Walliams, the BBC has removed several episodes, comedy sketches, and films featuring him, including his appearance in the Doctor Who episode "The God Complex." Unsurprisingly, the move has left fans online seething.

"The God Complex" is part of Matt Smith’s era on the British sci-fi show. In the episode, the Eleventh Doctor, along with his companions Amy Pond (Karen Gillan) and Rory Williams (Arthur Darvill), is trapped with a group of strangers in a 1920s hotel where each room contains someone’s greatest fear. The episode features Walliams as a cowardly, mole-like alien known as Gibbis, who later betrays the group of survivors.

Other programs featuring Walliams as either an actor or creator have also been removed. These include adaptations of his children’s books, such as Gangsta Granny, and an episode of the genealogy series, Who Do You Think You Are?

A BBC spokesperson addressed the removal of Walliams’ filmography, saying:

“These are serious allegations. As such, the BBC is looking carefully at all material featuring and written by David Walliams on our platforms. We are in the process of removing some programmes, including Little Britain, whilst this review is underway.”

Fan reaction to the removal of "The God Complex" has largely been negative on social media like Reddit.

“For fucks sake. You can’t just obsessively remove and re-edit every goddamn episode of television just because they happen to have some problematic people in them,” one redditor shared.

Other commenters noted that the BBC hadn’t removed content featuring other Doctor Who episodes featuring people who have faced allegations of sexual misconduct and harassment, such as Noel Clarke and John Barrowman, who played Mickey Smith and Jack Harkness, respectively, in the 2005 reboot. Earlier in October, Clarke pleaded not guilty to sexual offense charges.

This isn’t the first time the BBC has removed content for review following serious allegations. In 2024, the BBC removed "Fear Her," an episode featuring David Tennant as the Tenth Doctor and set during the Olympic Games, after BBC news anchor Huw Edwards was convicted of possessing indecent images of children. Edwards appeared in the episode as himself. The BBC responded by re-dubbing his voice.

It’s unclear how the BBC will handle Walliams' involvement in "The God Complex," but it seems unlikely the episode will remain unavailable forever. For now, one of Doctor Who’s best episodes is under lock and key, and may stay that way for a while.

Which Kind of Mattress Do Five-Star Hotels Use?

Most luxury hotels use hybrid mattresses in their suites to strike the right balance of softness and support, thanks to pocketed coils and layers of foam. These are also typically medium-firm mattresses since a plush mattress can be uncomfortable for some sleep positions like stomach sleepers. Ultra-firm beds are often too stiff for side sleeping and may not align with guests' expectations who are looking for soft mattresses to sink into on their vacations.

If you want to refresh your mattress without the hefty price tag of buying a new one, a mattress topper or featherbed could be a solid transitional pick. The Ritz-Carlton and W Hotels promote one made with white duck feathers, Mandarin Oriental’s exclusive topper comprises white goose feathers, Waldorf Astoria beds feature a down-alternative fill, and Marriott’s selection is polyester.

There’s no one-size-fits-all here, so it really comes down to your preferred loftiness and weight. Duck and goose feathers are heavier than others fills, though duck is slightly less fluffy and won’t trap heat as well. Down-alternatives, like polyester, are more allergen-friendly and “a good option for those who prefer to be vegan in their home,” Danielle Trotter, the former textiles technical designer at West Elm, previously shared.

What Is the Thickness of a Hotel Mattress?

Hotel mattresses usually average 12 inches in thickness. This makes it easier to hop in and out of bed, especially when using a box spring that can add extra height. Plus, it prevents the need for deep pocket sheets.

What Is the Difference Between Memory Foam and Latex?

Traditional memory foam has a body-contouring effect that allows your body to sink into your mattress, reducing motion transfer (a must for bedmates!). Meanwhile, latex foam offers more responsiveness (or bounce) and overall support. As Neslihan Ozdemir, founder of Girl on the Mattress, previously told AD, latex also holds up well over time and is temperature-regulating. Latex foam mattresses (and some memory foam options) are certified organic, making them eco-friendly foam picks, too.

Known as the most common mattress type, innerspring mattresses feature a bed of coils. There’s quite a range to these mattresses: Basic versions have a grid of steel coils, while premier options have a pillow top and pocketed coil technology, meaning each coil is individually wrapped to help with motion isolation. Ozdemir added that innerspring is best for people who sleep on the edge of the bed or are heavier in weight. If you prefer a more plush mattress, but still want the support of those coils, consider a hybrid mattress, which is layered with soft foam.
